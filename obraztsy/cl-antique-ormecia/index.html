Our Philosophy

"Each piece carries
the weight of centuries."

Ormecia was founded upon a singular belief: that the most profound objects ever created by human hands deserve more than a transaction. They deserve a reckoning. We do not simply sell antiques — we restore their rightful place in the narrative of civilisation, connecting each artifact with a collector worthy of its legacy.

Our curators work with the world's leading archivists, metallurgists, and art historians to ensure every piece in the Ormecia collection arrives with complete, verifiable provenance documentation — spanning from its creation to its current moment of acquisition.

Lot 0031 · French Empire

The Versailles
Mantle Clock

Paris, c. 1774 · Attrib. to Pierre-Philippe Thomire

Acquired from a private château in the Loire Valley, this exceptional ormolu and patinated bronze mantle clock bears all hallmarks of the Royal Manufacture period. Its movement, signed by the horologist Berthoud, has been conserved to full working condition. A document chain links this piece to the household of the Duchess of Orléans, establishing one of the most complete provenances in Ormecia's modern history.

The Ormecia Method

Authentication &
Curation Process

I

Discovery & Acquisition

Our international network of scholars, estate curators, and auction specialists surface objects of exceptional rarity from private collections, dormant estates, and institutional deaccessions across five continents.

II

Forensic Authentication

Every artifact undergoes metallurgical analysis, dendrochronology, pigment spectroscopy, and X-ray fluorescence examination — methods reserved for the world's foremost museum institutions.

III

Archival Research

Provenance investigators document the complete ownership chain — from commission or excavation through every subsequent collection, auction, and private transfer — creating an irrefutable historical record.

IV

Collector Acquisition

Acquisition is conducted through our Private Client Service — a discreet, white-glove transaction process including independent legal transfer, customs clearance, and specialist installation services.
