Common Inquiries

We maintain an elegant smart casual dress code. We kindly ask gentlemen to wear collared shirts and dress shoes. Jackets are recommended but not required. We do not permit athletic wear, flip-flops, or overly casual attire in the dining room.

We recommend booking at least two weeks in advance for dinner reservations, and four weeks for weekend evenings. For special occasions, holidays, or large parties, we suggest booking at least one month ahead. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar on a limited basis.

Absolutely. We take dietary requirements very seriously. Please inform us of any allergies, intolerances, or dietary preferences when making your reservation. Our kitchen can accommodate vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and most other dietary needs with advance notice. We will create a bespoke menu tailored to your requirements.

Our signature tasting menu consists of 7 courses and typically takes 2.5 to 3 hours to complete. We recommend allowing ample time to fully savor each course. If you have time constraints, please let us know when booking, and we can adjust the pacing accordingly.

We allow guests to bring their own wine with a corkage fee of $75 per bottle, limited to two bottles per table. We kindly ask that you bring wines not currently featured on our list. Our sommelier will be happy to assist with decanting and service.

Yes, we offer elegant gift certificates that can be purchased for any denomination. They are beautifully presented in our signature packaging and make an exceptional gift for any occasion. Please contact us directly or visit our restaurant to purchase.

Yes, our private dining room accommodates up to 16 guests and features its own dedicated service team. We also offer full restaurant buyouts for up to 32 guests. Custom menus, wine pairings, and floral arrangements can be arranged. Please contact our events team for availability and pricing.

We require 48 hours notice for cancellations or modifications to reservations. Cancellations made within 48 hours may be subject to a cancellation fee of $100 per guest. For parties of six or more, we require 72 hours notice. We appreciate your understanding as we prepare fresh ingredients specifically for your visit.
