Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 2010, Maison Rouge has established itself as a sanctuary for those who seek the extraordinary. Our philosophy is simple: source the finest seasonal ingredients, treat them with reverence, and present them with artistic vision.

Under the guidance of Executive Chef Antoine Moreau, each dish tells a story of terroir, tradition, and innovation. Our intimate 32-seat dining room ensures every guest receives our undivided attention.

Executive Chef

Antoine Moreau

Michelin Starred Chef

With over 20 years of culinary mastery across Paris, Lyon, and New York, Chef Moreau brings a profound respect for French gastronomic tradition while embracing contemporary innovation. His philosophy centers on the purity of ingredients and the poetry of presentation.

"Cooking is not just about sustenance. It is about memory, emotion, and the shared experience of beauty on a plate."
