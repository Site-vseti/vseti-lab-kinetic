The Hotel Experience

Facilities

Dining, drinks, and an indoor pool — everything you need without leaving the building.

Beyond the room

A good hotel is more than a place to sleep. At The Rivenstone, breakfast is taken seriously, the bar is a proper one, and the pool is quiet enough to use before anyone else is awake. Whether you're here for one night or several, these are the spaces that make a stay feel complete.

7:00 – 10:30

Restaurant & Breakfast Room

Breakfast is served daily in a bright room overlooking the street — a full buffet of pastries, fruit, and hot dishes, alongside a menu of cooked options made to order. By day the same room serves a relaxed all-day menu, from sandwiches and salads to something more substantial.

  • Continental buffet
  • Cooked breakfast menu
  • All-day dining
  • Dietary options on request
12:00 – 23:00

The Bar

A wood-panelled bar that works as well for a coffee and the newspapers as it does for an evening drink. The cocktail list runs from house classics to a short seasonal menu, and the fireplace lounge is a good spot to wind down after a day in the city.

  • Classic & seasonal cocktails
  • Light bites
  • Fireplace lounge
  • Open to non-residents
6:30 – 21:00

Indoor Pool

An eighteen-metre pool below ground level, kept quiet and warmly lit. Towels and loungers are provided, and early mornings tend to be the best time for a swim before the day starts. Open daily to all guests, free of charge.

  • 18-metre pool
  • Towels provided
  • Loungers & changing rooms
  • Complimentary for guests
