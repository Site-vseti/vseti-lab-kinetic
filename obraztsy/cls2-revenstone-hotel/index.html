Welcome

A quiet address in the heart of Marylebone

Behind a restored Victorian facade, The Rivenstone offers fifty-two rooms and suites, a wood-panelled bar, an indoor pool, and a restaurant known for its breakfast. It's the kind of hotel that suits both a long weekend and a working trip — calm, well run, and five minutes from the high street.

Our reception is staffed around the clock, and every member of the team is trained to make London feel a little easier, from the moment your taxi pulls up.
