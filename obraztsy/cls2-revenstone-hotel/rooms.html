Accommodation

Rooms & Suites

Three room types, each designed for a calm night's sleep and an easy morning.

Comfort, considered

Every room at The Rivenstone shares the same standard: a deep, well-dressed bed, blackout curtains, air conditioning, a proper desk, and a bathroom stocked with everything you'd hope to find. Where the rooms differ is in space, outlook, and the small touches that make a longer stay feel easy. Browse the three types below, then enquire — we're happy to talk through which suits your trip best.

Entry-level accommodation

Classic Room

A comfortable, well-proportioned room for one or two guests, with a city view over the street below. A good fit for a short business trip or a couple wanting a simple base for exploring London.

  • King or twin bed
  • City view
  • Work desk
  • En-suite bathroom
  • Air conditioning
  • Tea & coffee tray
